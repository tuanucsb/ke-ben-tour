# Kề Bên — product tour

Open the tour: https://tuanucsb.github.io/ke-ben-tour/

Screenshots and a two-minute film of the Kề Bên demo: post-discharge home care for Vietnamese
families. A hospital hands the patient over before discharge, a named coordinator accepts, and
a dated care plan runs at home.

Click through the screens yourself: https://tuanucsb.github.io/ke-ben-tour/app/
A read-only copy of the app's screens for each role. The links work; buttons that save do not.

Illustrative data. The hospitals, the clinic, patients, doctors and agreement numbers are
invented for this demo. They are not real organisations, people or signed partnerships. Care
pathways and alert thresholds are demo defaults that need clinician sign-off before use with
real patients.
