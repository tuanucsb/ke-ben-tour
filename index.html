Kề Bên.

Luôn kề bên người thân của bạn, từ bệnh viện về nhà

One app, three layers. The main one is post-discharge coordination: a hospital hands the patient over, a named coordinator accepts, and a dated care plan runs at home. Around it sit an open home-care marketplace (verified nurses, midwives, labs) and the family-health ecosystem (remote doctor, results, payments, one account for the whole family). Vietnamese-first.

Partner management and fees
Partners are paid a fixed fee for the documented handoff, to the facility, under a contract.
The rules block payment to individuals and keep percentage sharing locked until a written legal opinion is recorded. This design follows our reading of Vietnamese law and needs a Vietnamese healthcare lawyer's confirmation before any real payment.

7 · Ops console — desktop, the control roomOpen marketplace

30 · /ops
Intake and matching
Every booking across tracks, matched to a verified provider.
31 · /ops/escalations
Red-flag queue
Critical first. And the platform's position is written on the screen: it relays alerts to the responsible doctor; it does not diagnose or prescribe.
Safety triage plus the intermediary position (Decree 96/2023).
32 · /ops/payouts
Provider payouts and tax ledger
Every payout withholds 5% VAT and 2% PIT at source, with a running ledger.
Withholding works and is auditable (Decree 117/2025).
33 · /ops/providers
Provider credentialing
A provider whose practising certificate is not verified is never offered a job.
